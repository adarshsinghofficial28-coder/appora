Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately for upgrades, and at the end of the billing cycle for downgrades.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

Yes, Pro includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You'll have full access to all Pro features during the trial.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual Team plans.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period. No questions asked.
